Past and ongoing research projects.

Magnetism in 2D VSe2 and its charge density wave phase

2023 – 2025

National Institute of Standards and Technology · with Daniel Wines, Kamal Choudhary, and Francesca Tavazza

Benchmarked the material properties of the 2D transition metal dichalcogenide VSe2 across several density functionals to find the most reliable model, and studied how strain tunes its magnetic properties in both the ground state and the charge density wave state, with an eye toward spintronics applications.

Automated discovery of stable 2D alloys

2024 – 2026

National Institute of Standards and Technology

Developed a computational workflow that automates the generation, computation, and analysis of alloy structures, and applied it to VxTi1−xSe2 to find stable configurations with enhanced ferromagnetism. The workflow is planned for integration into JARVIS to enable high-throughput alloy studies with both DFT and machine-learning force fields.

Multiferroicity in 2D NiI2

2023 – 2025

UMBC STEAM Lab · advised by Can Ataca

Investigated monolayer NiI2 in its ground state to understand its multiferroic behavior and extract its magnetic interaction parameters, including a benchmark of how the choice of DFT functional affects those parameters.
